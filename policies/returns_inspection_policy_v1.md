SYNTHETIC POLICY FOR A FICTIONAL STORE.

## What inspection checks
Every returned item is inspected at our warehouse. We check that the item is the one sold, is complete with its accessories, and has no damage beyond normal handling.

## Failed inspection
If a returned item fails inspection (for example, it is missing parts, is damaged by the customer, or is a different item), it is not eligible for a refund. The customer may contact support to dispute the result, and a specialist will review it.

## Inspection pending
Inspection usually happens within 2 business days of the item arriving. A refund cannot be issued until inspection is complete.
