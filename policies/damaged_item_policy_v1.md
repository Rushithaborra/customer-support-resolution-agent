SYNTHETIC POLICY FOR A FICTIONAL STORE.

## Reporting damage
If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery. Reports after 7 days are not covered by this policy.

## Damage review
Every damage report is reviewed by a support specialist, who may ask for photos of the item and its packaging. The specialist decides between a replacement and a refund.
