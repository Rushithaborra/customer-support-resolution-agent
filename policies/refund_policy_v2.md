SYNTHETIC POLICY FOR A FICTIONAL STORE.

## When a refund can be issued
A refund is issued only after the returned item has been received at our warehouse and has passed inspection. If the return is still in transit, or has not yet arrived, no refund can be issued yet; the customer will be refunded once it arrives and passes inspection.

## Return window
Returned items must reach our warehouse within 30 days of delivery to be eligible for a refund. Items received after 30 days are not eligible.

## Refund amount and method
We refund the full amount paid for the returned item, in Indian rupees, to the original payment method. Partial refunds are not offered.

## Duplicate refunds
An order that has already been refunded, or has a refund in progress, cannot be refunded again.

## Refund processing time
After a refund is approved, our payment provider usually completes it within 5 to 7 business days. A refund is only confirmed to the customer once the payment provider reports it as completed.
