SYNTHETIC POLICY FOR A FICTIONAL STORE. Retired: replaced by version 2 on 1 July 2026.

## When a refund can be issued
A refund is issued only after the returned item has been received at our warehouse and has passed inspection. We do not refund an order while the return is still in transit.

## Return window
Returned items must reach our warehouse within 15 days of delivery to be eligible for a refund.

## Refund amount
We refund the full amount paid for the returned item, in the original payment method.

## Duplicate refunds
An order that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
