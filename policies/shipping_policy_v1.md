SYNTHETIC POLICY FOR A FICTIONAL STORE.

## Delivery times
Orders are usually delivered within 3 to 5 business days of shipping. Customers can follow their order with the carrier's tracking number.

## Delayed shipments
If an order has not arrived by its estimated delivery date, support will check the carrier's tracking and share the latest status with the customer.
